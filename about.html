— OUR CORPORATE JOURNEY

From Agricultural Roots to High-Speed Compact Engineering.

Incorporated on 23 March 2016 under the Indian Companies Act, Rudra Cottex Limited was founded to bridge the gap between Gujarat's premium agricultural yield and the uncompromising yarn specifications demanded by luxury apparel manufacturers worldwide.

Our operational ethos pairs zero-compromise raw fiber grading with automated Swiss blowroom technology, high-speed ring spinning frames, and automated optical clearing. By strictly adhering to Uster 5% quality tolerances, every package dispatched from our Gandhinagar mill represents peak tensile strength and minimal hairiness.
