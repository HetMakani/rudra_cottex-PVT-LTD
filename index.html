01 / Heritage • Excellence

A trusted textile institution built on technological purity and unyielding quality.

Incorporated on 23rd March 2016 in Gujarat, India, Rudra Cottex Limited has risen to prominence as one of western India's most advanced cotton spinning and trading enterprises. Operating across a sprawling 13,137 sq. m. manufacturing complex in Gandhinagar, we convert certified Shankar-6 and MCU-5 cotton fibers into yarn of superlative evenness and strength.

Our technological signature is EliTwist® twin-spun compact spinning—a revolutionary system that pneumatically condenses two rovings and twists them together directly at the ring spindle. This produces an ultra-smooth two-ply yarn with extreme seam tenacity and hairiness values (H < 3.2), answering the exacting standards of luxury European and Asian fashion houses.
