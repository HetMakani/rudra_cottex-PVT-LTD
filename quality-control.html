— SWISS PRECISION ANALYTICS

Uster Tester 6 & High-Volume Fiber Diagnostics.

We deploy Swiss Uster Tester 6 with Sensor CS for comprehensive yarn evenness, coefficient of variation (CVm%), thin places (-50%), thick places (+50%), and neps (+200%). Fiber elongation and single-yarn tenacity are verified continuously on digital Tensorapid testers.

Uster Tester 6 (Evenness & IPI)

Measures mass variation, hairiness index (H), and imperfections per 1,000 meters.

Tensorapid 4 Tensile Strength

Measures RKM, breaking force (cN/tex), and elongation at break (E%).

High Volume Instrument (HVI)

Pre-testing raw cotton bales for staple length, strength, micronaire, and trash content.

Classimat 5 Fault Analyzer

Classifies rare objectionable yarn defects (A1 to D4) to ensure high-speed airjet weaving stability.
