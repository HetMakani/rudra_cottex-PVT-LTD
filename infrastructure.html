CLIMATE & MICRO-ATMOSPHERE CONTROL

Luwa Precision Humidification & Air Filtration.

Cotton yarn elasticity and elongation are extraordinarily sensitive to ambient relative humidity. Our plant maintains continuous closed-loop RH calibration across every spinning shed:

Carding & Drawing: 55% ± 2% RH

Maintains fiber cohesion while preventing static buildup and nep formation.

Ring Spinning & Compact Zone: 62% ± 2% RH

Optimizes fiber friction for maximum tensile CSP and minimal end-breakage rates.

Winding & Conditioning: 68% ± 2% RH

Eliminates packaging static and ensures perfect cone moisture regain prior to export sealing.
